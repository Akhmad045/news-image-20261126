A broadsheet front page summing up Thursday 26 November 2026 in one
image. Lead: Russia's own leaked statistics put its war dead at
490,334 by September — with the true toll likely past 700,000. Also:
Pakistan's first astronaut reaches China's Tiangong station aboard
Shenzhou-24; North Korea expels the Swiss envoy and shuts its
Pyongyang office; 20 hostages are freed in Cartagena; a fuel tanker
fire kills 31 in Cairo; the 100th Macy's Thanksgiving Day Parade
fills the New York sky; and Duran Duran guitarist Andy Taylor dies
at 65.

`main.py` (Python 3.13, standard library only) copies `entry.png` to
`/output/entry.png`. The image is drawn at build time by `draw.py`
(Pillow) and is byte-identical on every run.
